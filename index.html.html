Introduction to AM

AM vs Conventional

Additive manufacturing builds parts layer by layer, enabling complex geometries with minimal waste — unlike subtractive (cutting) or formative (molding) processes.

Evolution & Standards

From rapid prototyping to production-grade parts. ASTM/ISO 52900 classifies 7 process categories: from Vat Photopolymerization to Directed Energy Deposition.

Generic Process Chain

CAD → STL → Slice → Build → Post-process. Digital workflow that transforms a 3D model into a physical component.

CO1 Learning outcome: Distinguish between additive, subtractive, and formative manufacturing. Classify AM processes per ASTM/ISO 52900.

Polymer-Based AM

FDM / FFF

Material extrusion: thermoplastic filament melted and deposited layer by layer. Key parameters: nozzle temp, bed adhesion, infill.

SLA / DLP

Vat photopolymerization: UV light cures liquid resin. High resolution, smooth surface finish.

SLS (Polymer)

Powder bed fusion: laser sinters polymer powder. No supports needed, good mechanical properties.

CO2 Compare FDM, SLA/DLP and SLS for polymers. Identify basic troubleshooting for build failures.

Metal-Based AM

SLM / DMLS

Powder bed fusion for metals. High-energy laser melts metal powder, building dense, complex parts.

DED

Directed Energy Deposition: metal powder or wire melted by laser/electron beam, used for repair and large parts.

Binder Jetting

Liquid binder selectively deposited on metal powder; green part later sintered.

CO3 Analyze and compare metal AM processes on cost, material, suitability. Understand microstructure & properties.

DfAM & Software Tools

Topology Optimization

Generative design: material placement based on load paths, reducing weight while maintaining strength.

Lattices

Periodic & stochastic cellular structures. Enable lightweight, energy-absorbing, and bio-inspired designs.

Slicing & Toolpath

CAD-to-print workflow: from STL to G-code. Hands-on slicing exercise with orientation and supports.

CO4 Apply DfAM principles including topology optimization and lattice generation in digital CAD workflows.
